# Larger few-distance sets in ℝ⁶, ℝ⁷, ℝ⁸ and ℝ⁹ from shells of root lattices

**Bryan Zachary Bu, Karam Gill, Hyunjun Yi**

September 2026.

DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.23343425](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23343425)

## Abstract

Szöllősi and Östergård tabulated the largest known s-distance sets for d ≤ 8 and called the entries for s = 5, 6 in dimensions 6 to 8 “rather weak”. The vectors of norm at most 6 in E₆, E₇ and E₈ contain much larger ones: 133 and 227 points in ℝ⁶ (s = 5, 6), 130, 253 and 388 points in ℝ⁷ (s = 4, 5, 6), and 258, 520 and 910 points in ℝ⁸, against the previously recorded 112, 140; 127, 168, 280; and 241, 252, 504. The same method gives 4- and 5-distance sets of 235 and 440 points in ℝ⁹. All sets are given explicitly as integer vectors and checked by an exact, dependency-free script.
